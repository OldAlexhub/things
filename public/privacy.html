Privacy policy

Things to Do is developed by Old Alex Hub. Contact: info@oldalexhub.com. Updated November 14, 2026.

The app communicates with our backend for content, recommendations and itinerary planning. Requests may include your selected city, interests, starting coordinates, date, budget and participant ages. No public account is required. This backend processes planning inputs without saving them as a user profile. Application logs record request paths, status and timing, not request bodies or credentials.

Favorites, saved itineraries, collections, visited places, searches and preferences are stored locally. Android device backups are disabled. Clear local data in Settings or uninstall to remove it. Delete exported files separately. Cached provider content expires. Expired saved entries retain only an identifier and unavailable-content label.

Approximate location is optional and requested only when you choose a nearby or starting-location feature. It may be sent to the backend to search or plan. There is no background location collection. Revoke permission in Android settings or enter a starting point manually.

Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform are integrated. Ads remain off until you confirm you are at least 18 and consent information permits ad requests. This app requests non-personalized ads. Providers may still process identifiers, IP address, coarse location, app interactions, diagnostics and ad performance information for delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and compliance. Non-personalized does not mean no data processing. Regional choices depend on the published Google consent configuration. Find advertising privacy choices in Settings. Ad or consent failures do not block the app.

Maps request visible tiles from the configured provider, initially OpenStreetMap, revealing IP address and viewed map area. No offline tile downloading is offered. Directions, booking and source links open external services with their own privacy policies.

Content may come from OpenStreetMap, licensed contributors and enabled event providers such as Ticketmaster. Source attribution appears with content. Imported records expire under configured provider retention. Synchronization logs default to 30 days, and admin audit entries to 90 days. Infrastructure may retain separate access logs according to the deployment configuration. Contact info@oldalexhub.com for questions or deletion requests about information held by Old Alex Hub.

The app is intended for adults planning activities, including family trips. Entered child ages support group suitability without names. Do not submit names or sensitive personal information. This app is not directed to children under 13. Ads stay off without adult confirmation.

Policy changes appear here and in the hosted policy. Before publication, the operator must confirm hosting retention, regional consent messages and Google Play Data safety disclosures match the deployed service.
